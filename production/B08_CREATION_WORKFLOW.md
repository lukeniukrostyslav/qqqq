# B08 — Asset Creation Workflow

Дата: 2026-09-27

## Production order

B08 will be executed in this order:

1. **Style proof**
2. Journal Pages
3. Digital Papers
4. Ephemera
5. Decorative PNG
6. Tags / Bookmarks
7. Bonus
8. Internal batch review
9. Handoff to B09

## 1. Style proof

**Style proof status: ACCEPTED on 2026-09-27 after rendered visual inspection.** Final asset files are still subject to individual QA.

Before creating the full 90-item batch, create a small representative proof:
- 1 journal page;
- 1 digital paper;
- 1 ephemera item;
- 1 decorative transparent PNG;
- 1 tag;
- 1 bookmark.

The six-piece proof is used to validate visual cohesion.

If the proof fails cohesion, revise the style before mass production.

## 2. Creation principles

Every asset must satisfy:
- same visual universe;
- readable composition;
- useful practical function;
- no filler;
- no copied competitor design;
- no recognizable third-party IP;
- no unexplained modern visual elements;
- clean edges where transparency is intended.

## 3. Raster standards

Journal pages:
- US Letter 2550×3300;
- A4 2480×3508;
- 300 DPI metadata where supported.

Digital paper:
- 3600×3600 target minimum;
- JPG delivery.

Transparent PNG:
- 3000 px+ longest side where practical;
- genuine alpha transparency;
- no white matte/halo.

These are the product's internal production standards, not claims about marketplace requirements.

## 4. Proof-before-batch rule

No mass production before the six-piece style proof is visually inspected.

The proof must answer:
- Do all six assets look like one collection?
- Is the palette coherent?
- Is the texture coherent?
- Are decorative motifs reused intelligently rather than mechanically?
- Are journal pages still usable for writing?
- Do transparent PNGs have clean edges?
- Does the collection look commercial rather than like a random AI/image dump?

## 5. Asset acceptance

An asset becomes APPROVED only when:
- content is correct;
- dimensions are correct;
- format is correct;
- filename is correct;
- transparency is correct if required;
- visual style is coherent;
- no obvious artifacts are present;
- originality/licensing check passes.
