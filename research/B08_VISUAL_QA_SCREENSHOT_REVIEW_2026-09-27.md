## Gap identified

Our current proof set lacks:
1. painterly/engraving-level object detail;
2. natural irregularity;
3. realistic paper/ink wear;
4. convincing illustrated books and architecture;
5. layered collage depth;
6. fine botanical/specimen detail;
7. varied edge treatment;
8. richer tonal separation;
9. visual storytelling;
10. enough distinctive artwork per page.

Adding more rectangles, lines, borders and simple silhouettes is **not** the solution.

## New production direction

The next proof must be **illustration-first**, not vector-layout-first.

For the next test:
- use generated/original illustrated artwork as the visual base;
- build the page around a real focal illustration;
- overlay typography and writing zones afterward;
- use scanned/painted-looking textures and imperfect print artifacts;
- combine 3–6 visual layers rather than geometric blocks;
- keep writing usability, but let the artwork establish the premium look.
