## Production decision
Create exactly 10 assets:
- 5 tags: practical writing/label surfaces with varied silhouettes.
- 5 bookmarks: tall, printable page markers with distinct library motifs.
- Avoid branded/franchise references and avoid meaningless color-only duplicates.
- Use original vector artwork consistent with the frozen B07 style guide.
- Delivery will be print-ready JPGs at 300 DPI, with clean readable compositions.
