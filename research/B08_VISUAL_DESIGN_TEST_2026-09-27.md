## Why this test exists

Current market checks show that competing dark-academia/library journal products commonly combine:
- aged paper/background treatment;
- archival/library typography;
- catalog cards, labels, stamps or book-spine details;
- layered ephemera;
- restrained but visible decorative framing.

## V2 design decision

The existing approved Library Catalogue page is intentionally clean and highly usable, but visually sparse relative to the current market examples.

V2 therefore adds depth without turning the writing page into a collage:
- subtle paper grain pattern;
- double archival frame;
- small circular archive seal;
- secondary metadata line;
- lightly tinted writing panel;
- subtle bookshelf silhouette;
- archival status block;
- restrained botanical line;
- small footer stamp.

Target visual balance:
- approximately 80–90% functional writing area;
- approximately 10–20% decorative/archival treatment.
