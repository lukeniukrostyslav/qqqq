# B08 Production Research — 2026-09-27

- Etsy supports JPG/PNG/SVG/HEIC for listing images; transparent PNGs are not supported as listing images. Etsy recommends listing images at least 2000 px and sRGB. Source: Etsy Help.
- Etsy digital listings allow up to five buyer-download files, max 20 MB each; ZIP is supported. Source: Etsy Help.
- Etsy states digital items must be made and/or designed by the seller. Source: Etsy Help.

Production decision: 20 journal-page concepts, US Letter + A4 variants, 300 DPI raster target, RGB/sRGB-compatible, JPG delivery, SVG source masters, no third-party imagery or recognizable franchise IP.

Listing-image requirements are separate from buyer product-file requirements.
